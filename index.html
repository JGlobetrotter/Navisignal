When teams use Navisignal

Use Scenarios

  • Assessing supplier readiness for compliance with global sustainability standards (e.g. CSRD, GRI and others.)
  • Mapping human rights due diligence responsibilities across supply chains
  • Evaluating whether to bid on a complex donor-funded proposal
  • Structuring go/no-go decisions and diagnostics under time and resource pressure
  • Drafting AI enabled M&E frameworks aligned with donor reporting standards

Output Examples

  • Structured logframes with automated indicator or KPI tracking
  • RACI matrices for governance and accountability
  • Compliance matrices mapped to donor requirements
  • Risk assessment scorecards for suppliers, sponsors and partner organizations
  • Decision briefs for institutional review and sign-off, leveraging applied AI
